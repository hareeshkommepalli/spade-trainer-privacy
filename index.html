SPADE Trainer — Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Short version: SPADE Trainer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you do in the app — lessons, progress, XP, streaks, flow-sheet notes — stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the SPADE Trainer app ("the app," "we," "us") handles your information. It applies to the app published on the Apple App Store.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

Where your data is stored

All app content and your progress are stored locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device storage. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data.

No network transmission, analytics, ads, or trackers

The app is designed to work fully on-device. It does not send data over the network, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies.

Notifications

If you opt in to daily practice reminders, those notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No information is transmitted to us or to any third party to deliver them. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your device's Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. To delete all of it, simply delete the app from your device. We retain nothing because we never receive anything.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for students, including those under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children. We do not build profiles or target advertising.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. The app is not integrated with third-party data-collection services.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above before the change takes effect.
